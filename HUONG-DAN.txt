TRIEN KHAI WEB TAM SU LEN VERCEL - ten mien tamsu360.com
=====================================================

CACH LAM (5 buoc):

1. Tao kho GitHub, tai toan bo thu muc tam-su-vercel len
   (index.html, package.json, vercel.json, thu muc api/).

2. Vao https://vercel.com, dang nhap, nhan Add New > Project,
   chon kho vua tao, nhan Deploy.

3. Tao kho du lieu: vao Storage > Create Database > KV (Upstash),
   chon gan vao project. Vercel tu nap bien moi truong.

4. Gan ten mien: vao Project > Settings > Domains,
   nhap tamsu360.com (ban phai so huu ten mien nay),
   lam theo huong dan tro DNS tu nha cung cap ten mien.

5. Mo https://tamsu360.com la dung. May chu that tren cloud,
   tat may ca nhan web van chay, khong can tunnel.

LUU Y:
- Ten mien tamsu360.com phai mua rieng (VD Namecheap, GoDaddy),
  Vercel khong cap mien .com mien phi.
- Neu chua mua mien, cu dung mien mien phi dang
  ten-project.vercel.app truoc.
