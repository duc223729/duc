TRIEN KHAI WEB TAM SU LEN VERCEL
==============================
1. Tai toan bo thu muc tam-su-vercel len kho GitHub.
2. Vao vercel.com > Add New > Project > chon kho > Deploy.
3. Vao Storage > Create > Upstash for Redis > dat ten tamsu-kv
   > Connect vao project.
4. Vao Deployments > Redeploy ban moi nhat.
5. Mo dia chi ten-project.vercel.app la dung.
Khong can mua ten mien. Tat may ca nhan web van chay.
